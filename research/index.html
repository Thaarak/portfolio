Research

I work on two problems: how to steer a climate model toward a goal, and how to tell whether an AI system did the job it was given.

Climate Analytics Lab

UC San Diego
Since March 2026
Advised by Duncan Watson-Parris

Marine cloud brightening is a proposed way to cool the planet by spraying sea-salt particles into low ocean clouds so they reflect more sunlight. An open question is how anyone would run it. Someone has to decide where and how much to brighten, week by week, when nobody knows exactly how strongly the clouds will respond.

I work on that problem inside JAX-GCM, the lab’s differentiable atmosphere model. Because the whole model is written in JAX, you can take gradients through weeks of simulated climate and use them to plan. I build planners that do this and compare them with the feedback controllers used in earlier geoengineering simulations.

We are writing the paper now, and I’ll say more here when it is out.

Data Science Teaching and Learning Lab

UC San Diego
Since October 2025
dstl.ucsd.edu

Instructors are starting to hand whole course updates to AI coding agents. The agent edits many files, and then someone has to find out what it did. We study that review step. We look at what agents change, what they miss, and what an instructor needs to see before trusting the result.

Our paper, The Edit Is Not the End, introduces CourseLens, a review workspace that lays an agent’s edits out in teaching order and shows which files it changed, checked, or never opened. It will appear at SIGCSE TS 2027 in Sacramento.

Before college

Quest Science Center, Livermore
UC Santa Barbara
2023 to 2025

In high school I worked on citizen-science projects through Quest Science Center in Livermore. We built fixed and mobile temperature sensors to map the city’s heat islands, and surveyed its buildings in near-infrared to look for wasted energy. We presented both at AGU annual meetings.

In the summer of 2024, at UC Santa Barbara’s Summer Research Academies, Haeum Lee, Simran Karkhanis and I studied the social barriers to agrivoltaics, the practice of farming under solar panels.
