## Decisions

White `#ffffff`, ink `#272d31`, secondary text `#626b72`, links `#285b80`, separators `#dde2e5`, image backing `#f4f6f7`. Georgia for reading; Arial for navigation, dates, and captions. The main column is 968px inside a 1056px shell; prose stays narrower. At phone sizes the layout becomes a single column and navigation wraps without a hidden menu.

No animation, gradients, logo grids, fabricated metrics, or daily data feature. No repository-rejection jokes. Public projects are presented with individual contribution and team attribution. Small onboarding projects are explicitly labeled.
