Hi, I’m Thaarak.

I study data science and physics at UC San Diego. My work spans climate modeling, human–computer interaction, and AI evaluation.

I’m interested in how we understand complex systems, and how we make the tools built around them useful and trustworthy.

That takes a few forms: simulating weather interventions, building interfaces people can inspect, and working on climate education in my community.

What I’m working on

I do research in climate modeling and human–computer interaction, alongside building small software tools. I work primarily with Python, JAX, TypeScript, and React.

Climate work, closer to home

Before UC San Diego, I helped organize the Livermore Youth Climate Summit and establish the Tri-Valley Youth Climate Action Program. With three other students, I helped develop a climate-literacy resolution adopted by our school district in October 2024.

The coverage page brings together reporting on that work, award announcements, and conversations.
