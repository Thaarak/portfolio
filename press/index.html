Most of what has been written about me covers climate work in Livermore, my hometown. I did all of it with other students and with mentors at Quest Science Center, so each story below is about a group.

2025

Mapping Livermore’s heat

Livermore had no street-level heat data, so a group of us in the Tri-Valley Youth Climate Action Program built temperature sensors and mapped the city ourselves. We found differences of up to 14°F between neighborhoods and took the results to the city’s sustainability staff. Shrish Premkrishna and I told that story at the Climate Storytellers’ Summit, hosted by programs at Yale, Penn and Princeton.

2024

A climate literacy resolution

Claire Gresham, Alexander Pepper, Shrish Premkrishna and I wrote a climate literacy resolution for the Livermore Valley Joint Unified School District. We met with board members, the superintendent and the teachers’ association while drafting it, and the board adopted it on October 15, 2024.

2023–24

The Livermore Youth Climate Summit

In the summer of 2023 a group of us organized Livermore’s first Youth Climate Summit with Quest Science Center. That fall the planning team received the Tri-Valley 2040 Regional Visionary Award at Innovation Tri-Valley’s DreamMakers & RiskTakers awards.
