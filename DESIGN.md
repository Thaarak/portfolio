# Design notes, second version

## What changed and why

The first version was tidy but read like a template: a "Hi, I'm Thaarak" headline, label-and-content sections, nine project pages with the same four headings, and thirteen coverage entries with newspaper-page thumbnails. The copy stayed abstract, and the research page left out the accepted paper.

This version is modeled on the way https://www.mrinanksharma.net/ works, not on how it looks:

- **The home page is a photograph and a few paragraphs.** No headline. Every proper noun is a link, so the prose is the navigation.
- **Each page is one facet of a person**, opened by a single large sentence. Page titles are left to the navigation.
- **One typeface does everything.** Newsreader, self-hosted, with optical sizing, so the same family is delicate at display sizes and sturdy in text. Dates and labels are italic or small letterspaced capitals, never a second font.
- **Warm paper, one accent.** Paper `#faf7f0`, ink `#1d1c19`, secondary text `#6b655a`, hairlines `#d9d2c3`, links in the forest green of the Youth Climate Summit shirts, `#1f5a49`. Two ink-colored rules frame each page; everything else is a hairline.

Other references looked at: ethanperez.net (short bio, then a plain list), macwright.com (dated lists doing the work of a layout), patrickcollison.com (prose first).

## Page decisions

- **About.** Bio, a dated "Lately" list for what is current, and one pull quote: a line from the Climate Storytellers' Summit talk, in Thaarak's own words.
- **Research.** Lab name and dates in the margin, prose beside it, then a bibliography with years in the margin. The marine cloud brightening work is described by topic only until the paper is out.
- **Projects.** One page, seven entries, no detail pages. Each entry is a figure, what the thing does, which part was Thaarak's, an honest caveat, and links. Projects without a screenshot get a real artifact as their figure: the paper's title block for CourseLens, the demo's output table for the solar pipeline. Onboarding exercises sit in a short "Smaller things" list.
- **Press.** Grouped by story, not by outlet or type. Each story has a short account in the first person, then its sources. Photographs appear once per story, not once per article.

## Kept out on purpose

Animation, gradients, cards, icons, logo grids, a second typeface, client-side JavaScript, third-party requests, unpublished results, and private repository contents.

## Open for iteration

- All first-person copy is a draft for Thaarak to rewrite in his own voice.
- The research page has no figure yet. A plot or photograph that is cleared for public use would help.
- The portrait is a studio headshot; a less formal photograph would suit the tone better.
