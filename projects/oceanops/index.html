OceanOps

A shipboard planning prototype for exploring ocean alkalinity, plume simulations, and fleet routes.

Source codeTry the interactive demo

The project

OceanOps lets you change a vessel’s speed, discharge rate, or feedstock and compare the modeled plume. A map brings together sample fleet routes, marine traffic, and protected areas.

My contribution

My hackathon contributions included NATS fleet telemetry with an HTTP fallback, AI analysis, map overlays, simulation flow, and fixes to route rendering.

Context

Built with Akshat Choudhari and Yashmit Bhaverisetti at LA Hacks 2026. Winner of the ASUS Challenge.

This is a planning prototype. The public demo’s chemistry thresholds, plume, and carbon estimates are illustrative, not operational guidance.
